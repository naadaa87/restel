Plenty of flights into Seoul land early in the morning. You step out of Incheon after a night in a cramped seat, your body thinks it's the middle of the night, and your room may not be ready until the afternoon. The first day can easily slip away in a haze.

Here is a gentle plan for that day. It isn't medical advice — just the approach many travellers find works.

## 1. Daylight and breakfast first

Once you've cleared the airport, get outside if you can. Morning light is one of the most commonly recommended ways to nudge your body clock towards local time. Then eat something warm. A bowl of soup or a simple Korean breakfast goes a long way after airline food.

## 2. Head into the city on the AREX

The AREX airport railway runs from both Incheon terminals into Seoul. The all-stop train reaches Hongik University Station (Hongdae) in around an hour from Terminal 1 — a good base if you're staying on the west side of the city. Sinchon is one stop further on subway Line 2.

## 3. A short nap, not a long sleep

This is the step most people get wrong. When you're exhausted, a long sleep in the middle of the day feels irresistible, but it often leaves you wide awake at 3 a.m. The usual advice is to keep a first-day nap short, around 20–30 minutes, and then get up.

Lying down somewhere quiet and dim, with your phone out of reach and a gentle alarm set, makes that much easier than dozing on a café chair.

RESTEL Sinchon opens at 07:00, so you can book a Nap 60 even for an early arrival. Take your shoes off, lie down on a mat with a blanket, turn the lights to the lowest setting and set your alarm for half an hour. Use the remaining time to wash your face, drink some water and have a cup of tea before heading out. If you send your flight's arrival time with your request, we can suggest a start time that fits.

## 4. An easy walk in the afternoon

Keep the afternoon simple. Walking outdoors keeps you awake without wearing you out. From Sinchon you can stroll to Yonsei's leafy campus or along Hongdae's Gyeongui Line Forest Park — our [Sinchon, Hongdae and Ewha route](/en/journal/sinchon-hongdae-ewha-rest) has ideas.

## 5. An early night, on local time

Try to stay up until a reasonable local bedtime, even if it's an early one. Eat dinner, keep the evening quiet and turn in. By the second morning, most people feel much more like themselves.

## If you're also flying out late

The same approach works on your last day in reverse. If your flight leaves at night, a rest in the afternoon makes the journey home far easier. See [Late flight out of Seoul?](/en/journal/before-late-flight-seoul)
