Some days you rest for an hour and come out foggier than you went in. Usually it's because you looked at your phone, lay down somewhere too bright, or fell into a deep sleep and woke up in the middle of it. Resting well takes a little bit of order.

## 1. Put your phone out of reach

The first thing to do is turn your phone face down. If you lie down holding it, one notification pulls you straight back into your day. Set an alarm, then put it somewhere you can't see the screen and can't reach without getting up.

## 2. Lower the lights more than you think

You can sense brightness even with your eyes closed. In a rest space, turn the lights down to the lowest setting. At RESTEL Sinchon there are three levels; at the lowest, only the soft glow of the moon-gate niche remains.

## 3. Sit for the first five minutes

Rather than lying down the moment you arrive, sit with a warm cup of tea for five minutes. Your breathing settles after the walk, and the noise outside starts to fade. Putting on a guided meditation and simply counting breaths works well too.

## 4. Decide how long you'll nap

Length matters. A short nap is commonly kept to around 20–30 minutes; sleep much longer and you're more likely to wake from deep sleep feeling groggy. If you want a longer sleep, some people aim for about 90 minutes instead. Everyone is different, so a bit of trial and error is the most reliable guide.

Choose a gentle alarm — vibration or a quiet tone. Jolting awake can make the whole rest feel wasted.

## 5. Leave ten minutes to wake up

Don't rush out the second you open your eyes. Drink a glass of water, stretch, splash cold water on your face at the basin. If there's a terrace, step out for a breath of air. Then you're ready for the street again.

## A simple plan for 60 minutes and 3 hours

**Nap 60 (60 minutes)**

- 0–5 min: shoes off, phone face down, a cup of tea
- 5–40 min: lights at the lowest, lie down for a nap of around 30 minutes
- 40–55 min: water, a stretch, wash your face
- 55–60 min: fold the blanket, tidy up, head out

**Rest 180 (3 hours)**

- First 20 min: tea, then 10 minutes of guided meditation
- Next 90 min: a longer sleep, or just lying still
- Next 40 min: fresh air on the terrace, a light snack
- Last 30 min: read or write, then tidy up

None of this is a rule. But if you go in with no plan, the time vanishes; a rough order means you actually come out rested.

## Resting with someone else

If you've come with others, agree beforehand to chat for the first ten minutes and then rest quietly. Leave a little space between mats, and if someone is awake, they can use the table or the terrace.
