Sinchon is Seoul's great university district. Yonsei sits to the north, Ewha to the east and Hongdae to the west, each roughly one stop apart on subway Line 2. It's a wonderful area to explore on foot — and that is exactly why, by three in the afternoon, your legs tend to give up before your plans do. This route is built around that moment.

## Morning: Yonsei University and the Ansan trail

From Sinchon Station, walk north along Yonsei-ro for about ten minutes and you reach Yonsei's main gate. Follow the main avenue, Baekyang-ro, up into the campus; at the end stands Underwood Hall, one of the old stone buildings framed by trees. It's a lovely, quiet walk early in the day.

If you want more, look for the paths that lead up behind the campus to Ansan, a small mountain in Seodaemun. The Ansan Jarak-gil is a gently graded wooden deck that loops around the mountainside, so you don't need hiking boots. There are spots where the city opens up below you, and an old temple, Bongwonsa, sits on the lower slopes.

## Lunchtime: towards Ewha

Ewha Womans University is close enough to walk to from Sinchon. Inside the main gate, the ECC is a building sunk deep into the ground, with a walkway running through it like a valley. It's a working campus, so keep your voice down during class hours.

The streets in front of Ewha are full of small cafés and casual places for lunch.

## Mid-afternoon: stop and rest

This is the point where one proper rest makes the whole evening easier. A café works, but if you've been walking since morning, lying down with your legs stretched out feels much better.

RESTEL Sinchon is about five minutes from Sinchon Station. You take your shoes off, step up onto the wooden platform and lie down on a mat, and the noise of the city drops away a little. Nap 60 is enough for a short sleep; if you want to talk and rest with friends, Rest 180 gives you three easy hours. Brew a cup of tea in the tea corner, step out onto the terrace for some air, and you're ready for the evening.

## Evening: Hongdae and the Gyeongui Line Forest Park

One stop west on Line 2 is Hongik University Station. After dark, Hongdae's streets fill with buskers and crowds. For a calmer walk, head out of Exit 3 and follow the Yeonnam-dong section of the Gyeongui Line Forest Park, an old railway line turned into a long, narrow park.

If you're leaving Seoul soon, remember that Hongik University Station is also on the AREX airport line. We wrote a separate piece on [spending the afternoon before a late flight](/en/journal/before-late-flight-seoul).

## Getting around

- Sinchon (Line 2) ↔ Hongik University (Line 2, AREX, Gyeongui–Jungang): one stop
- Sinchon ↔ Ewha Womans University (Line 2): one stop
- Sinchon Station → Yonsei main gate: about 10 minutes on foot along Yonsei-ro
- Sinchon Station → RESTEL Sinchon: about 5 minutes on foot (exact location in your confirmation)
