The last day of a trip has an awkward shape. You have to leave your hotel in the morning, but your flight isn't until late at night. Dragging a suitcase around the city is tiring; going to the airport at lunchtime means hours on a bench.

Here is a calmer way to spend that afternoon, in Hongdae and Sinchon — right on the way to the airport.

## Work backwards from your flight

Start at your departure time and count back.

1. **Arrive at the airport.** For international flights, airlines usually ask you to be there two to three hours before departure. Check what yours recommends.
2. **Travel time.** From Hongik University Station, the AREX all-stop train reaches Incheon Airport Terminal 1 in around an hour, and Terminal 2 a little later. Gimpo Airport is much closer.
3. **A buffer.** Add about 30 minutes for walking to the station, buying a ticket and waiting for the train.

So for an 11 p.m. departure, you'd aim to reach the airport around 8 p.m., which means catching a train from Hongik University Station between about 6:30 and 7 p.m. Everything before that is time you can spend resting.

## Sort out your bags first

If you have a large suitcase, solve that before anything else. Major stations have coin lockers, and there are services that will carry your luggage to the airport ahead of you. If you'd like to bring bags to RESTEL Sinchon, add a note to your booking request and we'll tell you what works for the space.

## How much time do you have?

**About three hours:** book Rest 180. Make a cup of tea, turn the lights right down and sleep for an hour or so. When you wake up, wash your face at the basin, change into comfortable clothes for the flight and sort through your trip photos.

**One or two hours:** Nap 60. A short sleep makes the wait at the airport feel a lot shorter.

**Most of the day:** Day Rest gives you up to eight hours within opening times. Every session ends before midnight, so if your flight leaves in the small hours, give yourself plenty of time to reach the airport.

## Why not just wait at the airport?

Incheon has places to sit and rest, and it's a pleasant airport. But arriving very early usually means several hours in a chair. Resting quietly in the city and leaving at the right time makes a real difference to how you feel when you board. And your last memory of Seoul won't be a departure-gate seat.

Sinchon Station is one stop from Hongik University Station on Line 2. If you want to fill the morning too, see [A slow day between Sinchon, Hongdae and Ewha](/en/journal/sinchon-hongdae-ewha-rest).
